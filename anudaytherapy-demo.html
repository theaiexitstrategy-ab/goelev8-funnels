What We Offer

Therapy That Meets You Where You Are

01

Individual Therapy

For depression, anxiety, self-esteem, suicidal thoughts, and self-harm behaviors. We build on your strengths to help you find freedom from the darkness.

02

Couples Counseling

Using Emotionally Focused Therapy to help partners reconnect, repair ruptures, and build a relationship grounded in safety and trust.

03

DBT Group Therapy

Dialectical Behavior Therapy groups for people with suicidal ideation and self-harm — learning real skills to manage emotions and create a life worth living.

04

Crisis Counseling

Immediate, compassionate support for people in acute crisis. We're trained in Applied Suicide Intervention Skills — meeting you with no judgment, only care.

05

Dance & Movement Therapy

Your body holds stories your words can't always reach. Expressive arts and movement-based approaches can unlock healing that traditional talk therapy sometimes can't access alone.

06

Parenting Classes

Nurturing Parenting curricula for parents working toward family reunification — building connection, consistency, and confidence in the parenting role.

What We Offer

Services Built Around You

No two people heal the same way. That's why we blend clinical rigor with creative flexibility to find the approach that actually works for you.

01

Individual Therapy

Person-centered, strengths-based sessions for depression, anxiety, self-esteem, suicidal thoughts, and self-harm. We build on your strengths and empower you to heal from pain on your own terms.

02

Couples Counseling

Drawing from Emotionally Focused Therapy and Motivational Interviewing to help couples reconnect, repair ruptures, and build patterns of real safety and intimacy.

03

DBT Group Therapy

Dialectical Behavior Therapy groups for suicidal ideation and self-harm — building practical skills to manage emotions, tolerate distress, and create a life genuinely worth living.

04

Crisis Counseling

Immediate support for people in acute crisis. Our clinicians are trained in Applied Suicide Intervention Skills — meeting you exactly where you are with no judgment, only care and competence.

05

Dance & Movement Therapy

Your body knows things your words can't always reach. Movement and expressive arts techniques help clients access healing pathways beyond traditional talk therapy.

06

Parenting Classes

Nurturing Parenting curricula for parents working toward family reunification goals — building connection, confidence, and consistency in the parenting role.

For Mental Health Professionals

Training & Consultation

We offer specialized training for clinicians in Suicide Awareness & Prevention, Applied Suicide Intervention Skills (ASIST), Crisis Intervention, and other psychoeducational approaches. Equip your team with tools that save lives.
