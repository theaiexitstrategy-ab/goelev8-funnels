Meet Adrianne

Adrianne Martin, MSW, LCSW

I'm a Licensed Clinical Social Worker in private practice, specializing in work with people carrying thoughts of suicide and self-harm. I use person-centered therapy that builds on each client's existing strengths, rather than treating them as a diagnosis to manage.

My background in creative and performing arts shapes how I work — I bring movement, dance, and expressive techniques into sessions to help clients access and heal what words alone can leave stuck.

I've worked across the full continuum of care: crisis and residential facilities, psychiatric units, and suicide prevention hotlines, with individuals of every age, race, and diagnosis. I also teach parenting classes for parents working toward reunification with their children, and lead DBT groups for people managing suicidal and self-harm behaviors.
