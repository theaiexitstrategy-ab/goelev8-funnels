Common Questions

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW.

Is my card charged immediately when I sign up? +
No. Your card is authorized on Day 1 to verify it's valid, but no money moves until Day 8. If you cancel before Day 8, the authorization releases and you pay nothing. The 7-day trial is completely free with full access to your chosen plan.
What happens if I run out of SMS credits? +
Your SMS sequences pause automatically and you'll receive an email alert when your balance drops below 50 credits. No leads are lost — they stay in your CRM and sequences resume the moment you top up. Credits never expire so you can buy in bulk and use over time.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? +
Yes, anytime from your billing settings. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle. Moving from Grow to Launch will disable your AI phone agent and SMS blasts but your leads, funnels, and SMS credits are always preserved.
What is A2P 10DLC and do I need to do anything? +
You don't have to do anything. A2P 10DLC is the FCC's registration requirement for business SMS in the US. GoElev8.ai handles the entire registration process automatically when you activate a paid plan. All client SMS runs under our master Twilio account with full compliance handled for you — this is a competitive advantage we market as "we handle compliance" because most platforms make you figure it out yourself.
Does the AI phone agent actually call in 5 minutes? +
Yes — on the Grow and Scale plans, the system triggers an outbound call via Vapi within 5 minutes of a lead opting in. The agent uses SPIN selling methodology, is trained on your specific business using the information from your prompt, handles your industry's top objections, and books the lead on your calendar. Call this number right now to hear it: 888-302-0649
I already have a website. Do I need to replace it? +
No. Your existing Squarespace, WordPress, Wix, or Shopify site stays exactly as-is. GoElev8.ai connects to it via OAuth — no code, no copy-paste. We inject a lead capture widget automatically. Your site keeps its design. GoElev8 handles every lead that comes through it. The GoElev8 funnel page at goelev8.ai/f/your-slug is an additional acquisition channel, not a replacement.
What's the product store fee and how does it work? +
GoElev8.ai takes 10% of each product sale (8% on Scale) via Stripe Connect. You connect your Stripe account via OAuth — we never touch your funds directly. Stripe splits the payment automatically: your cut deposits to your bank instantly, our fee is collected at the same moment. Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Stripe Link are automatically enabled on all product checkouts. Full details in our Terms of Service.
Can I cancel at any time? +
Yes. Cancel anytime from your billing settings — no cancellation fees, no questions asked. Your data (leads, call transcripts, funnels) is retained for 30 days after cancellation so you can export it. After 30 days it's permanently deleted per our privacy policy.
