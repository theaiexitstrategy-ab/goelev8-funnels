Meeting You Where You Are
in the Journey

Throughout our lives we experience many ups and downs, changes, and transitions. When we experience these alongside trauma, anxiety, or depression, the challenges can feel especially overwhelming. Corine believes the support, guidance, and tools we gather along the way are what carry us through.

Her goal as a therapist is to support her clients in finding what works for them when navigating these difficult moments. She uses a client-centered approach — meeting you exactly where you are — while incorporating Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and Motivational Interviewing to support the work.

Especially for teens & young adults: If you're experiencing anxiety, looking for help with boundary setting, or building confidence through life transitions, Corine is passionate about building a therapeutic relationship that gives you real tools to move forward — with confidence and strength.

In Corine's Words

"Healing is often not linear and can be challenging — but I hope to provide you with the tools and support you need to tackle these challenges with confidence and strength. You are not alone in this process."

Her Therapeutic Approach

Corine's style is client-centered at its core — she follows your lead, respects your pace, and adapts the work to what you actually need rather than a preset formula.

She integrates Cognitive Behavioral Therapy to help identify and shift unhelpful thinking patterns, and Motivational Interviewing to help clients tap into their own reasons for change. The combination gives her flexibility to meet a wide range of needs without rigidity.

Most importantly: she believes reaching out — even when it feels overwhelming — is always worth it. Healing isn't easy, but you don't have to do it alone.
