Rooted in Crisis.
Grounded in Humanity.

Sylvia's background is rooted in crisis intervention with a strong passion for harm reduction. She specializes in trauma, grief, and addiction — and her approach is about meeting people where they are, on a human level.

Having personally experienced loss and trauma, she understands how isolating and stigmatizing that journey can be. She doesn't see herself as above her clients — she's alongside you, creating a space where you can be completely honest without fear of judgment.

In Sylvia's Words

"I truly believe I can't help with problems I don't know about, so transparency is key. Together, we'll work through whatever life throws your way with compassion and understanding."

Her Approach

Sylvia uses brief intervention therapy techniques — focused, practical, and grounded in where you are right now. She's known for her authenticity: what you see is what you get, and she expects the same openness from the therapeutic relationship.

Steadfast in her support and advocacy for clients, she believes in going the extra mile to provide appropriate and effective services — not just checking boxes.
