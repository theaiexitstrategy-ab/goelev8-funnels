Speaking Their Language.
Changing Their Story.

Kim brings 18 years of experience working with youth — with deep expertise in teenagers struggling with school behaviors, emotional regulation, connection, and the weight of growing up in a complex world. Her experience in a Special Needs School District gave her an exceptional ability to reach teens where they are.

She uses a trauma-informed approach to assist youth with crisis behaviors, substance-use counseling, self-esteem development, and executive functioning in the classroom. She speaks their language — and that's not a cliché. It's how she builds the trust that makes real therapeutic work possible.

"Kim is an incredible advocate for clients and engages them in change behaviors in order to reach a potential that they did not see in themselves. She is compassionate and caring with just the right amount of challenging to see youth and young adults grow."

About Kim's Work

Currently working toward her LCPC licensure under the supervision of Adrianne Martin, LCSW — deepening her clinical expertise while already delivering powerful results for the young people she serves.

Areas of Focus
Where Kim Makes the Difference
🎒

Teen Behavioral Issues

Struggles at school, conflict at home, acting out — Kim digs past the behavior to find the unmet need underneath. Then she helps teens build the skills to meet it in healthier ways.

🛡️

Trauma-Informed Care

Every behavior has a history. Kim approaches youth through a trauma-informed lens — understanding that challenging behaviors are often trauma responses, not character flaws.

🚨

Crisis Behaviors

Skilled in de-escalation and supporting youth through behavioral crises — with the ability to remain calm, connected, and effective even in high-intensity moments.

🧪

Substance-Use Counseling

Non-judgmental support for teens navigating substance use — understanding the role it plays in their world while building motivation for healthier coping strategies.

💪

Self-Esteem Development

Helping young people discover strengths they didn't know they had — and believe in a version of themselves that's genuinely worth becoming.

📚

Executive Functioning

Supporting teens with organization, focus, planning, and follow-through — bridging the gap between what they're capable of and what they're currently achieving in the classroom.

Her Approach

Kim pulls from a variety of therapeutic methods — always filtered through a trauma-informed lens. She's skilled in connecting with teenagers on their level, using their language, their references, and meeting them without judgment.

After 18 years in the field — including work in a Special Needs School District — she understands that every student who walks into a school or a session has a story. She helps them write a better next chapter.
